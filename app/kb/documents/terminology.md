# Refinery Terminology Glossary (Illustrative Reference)

> Illustrative reference material written for the UrjaKavach prototype.

**CUI** — Corrosion Under Insulation. Metal loss occurring beneath
insulation cladding, often undetected during external visual inspection.

**Flange joint** — bolted connection between two pipe sections, sealed by
a gasket. A common site of weeping and early seal failure.

**Rated operating pressure** — the design pressure envelope for a pipeline
segment. Sustained operation above this value accelerates fatigue.

**Peak load** — the period of maximum throughput in a unit, when pressure
and temperature readings typically reach their highest values.

**Maintenance window** — a scheduled period during which a unit or segment
is taken offline for planned intervention.

**Turnaround (TAR)** — a major scheduled shutdown for comprehensive
inspection and overhaul, typically every three to five years.

**Weeping** — slow seepage at a joint, below the threshold of a confirmed
leak but indicating seal degradation.

**Thickness monitoring location (TML)** — a fixed point on a pipe where
wall thickness is measured repeatedly over time to trend metal loss.

**P&ID** — Piping and Instrumentation Diagram. The schematic showing
equipment, piping, and instrumentation tags for a unit.

**Nominal** — a reading within expected design limits, requiring no action.

**Vibration tolerance** — the acceptable vibration amplitude band for
rotating equipment. Readings outside this band indicate misalignment,
imbalance, or bearing wear.
