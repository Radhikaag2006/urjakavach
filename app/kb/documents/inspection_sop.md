# Piping Inspection SOP (Illustrative Reference)

> Illustrative reference material written for the UrjaKavach prototype.
> Replace with your organisation's actual (non-confidential) SOP text
> before a production deployment.

## Scope

Covers routine external visual inspection and thickness monitoring of
process piping in refinery units.

## Finding Categories

Every observation recorded during inspection must be classified into
exactly one category:

- **Corrosion** — external or internal metal loss, including under-insulation
  corrosion (CUI). Record location, extent, and whether metal loss is
  measurable.
- **Gasket / Seal Wear** — degradation at flange joints, visible extrusion,
  weeping, or early signs of seal failure.
- **Pressure Deviation** — any gauge reading outside the rated operating
  envelope. Record gauge tag and percentage deviation.
- **Temperature Excursion** — sensor readings outside design limits.
- **Vibration Anomaly** — rotating equipment vibration outside tolerance.
- **Leak Detection** — any confirmed active leak. Always Critical severity.
- **Insulation Damage** — compromised cladding or lagging.
- **Structural / Support Defect** — damaged pipe supports, hangers, shoes.

## Severity Classification

- **Observation** — no action required, recorded for trend tracking.
- **Minor** — action required at next scheduled maintenance window.
- **Major** — action required within 30 days; supervisor notification.
- **Critical** — immediate action; active leak, or deviation above 10%.

## Pressure Deviation Thresholds

| Deviation above rated pressure | Severity |
|---|---|
| Up to 2% | Observation |
| 2% to 5% | Minor |
| 5% to 10% | Major |
| Above 10% | Critical |

## Equipment Tagging Convention

- Pipeline segments: `PL-` followed by three digits and a section letter,
  e.g. `PL-204B`.
- Pressure gauges: `PG-` followed by two digits, e.g. `PG-11`.
- Pumps: `P-` followed by digits, e.g. `P-7`.
- Heat exchangers: `E-` followed by three digits, e.g. `E-301`.
- Columns: `C-` followed by three digits, e.g. `C-102`.
